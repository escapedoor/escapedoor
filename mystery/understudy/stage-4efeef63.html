The Gentleman on the Terrace

An old comedy, and a new first night. Miss Lascelles has never been better. In the long speech she stopped, opened her fan, and thought; and a hundred and eighty-six of us stopped breathing. I am told that her necklace was glass. I did not notice, and neither will you.
