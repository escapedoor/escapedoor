How to give an order
  1. IT is the last thing you named. If you mean the hook, name the hook last.
  2. THEM wants things named together, with AND.
  3. UNTIL goes on, and stops when the thing comes true. WHILE does nothing unless the thing is true already.
  4. AS SOON AS waits. AFTER is fine. BEFORE is too late, if the thing has happened.
  5. IF acts when the thing is true. UNLESS acts when it is not.
  6. ALL BUT ONE leaves the last. If you mean another one, say which.
  7. Orders are done in the order given. Light first; then the picture.
