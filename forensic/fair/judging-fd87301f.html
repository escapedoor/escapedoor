CASE CLOSED

What you now know
  1. A fingerprint is mostly sweat, left by the ridges of the skin. Fine powder sticks to it and shows it up.
  2. There are three patterns: an arch has no delta, a loop has one and a whorl has two. Loops are the commonest and arches the rarest.
  3. A pattern can rule a person out. It can never say who. For that you compare ridge details: ridge endings, bifurcations and islands, one at a time.
  4. Each test answers one question. Vinegar makes bicarbonate of soda fizz. Iodine turns starch blue-black. Salt crystals are cubes. Sugar melts and goes brown.
  5. Black ink is a mixture of dyes. Water carries each dye up the paper at its own speed, and so pulls the mixture apart: chromatography.
  6. Rf = distance moved by the dye ÷ distance moved by the solvent. The same ink in the same solvent always gives the same Rf values, however long the strip is.
  7. Evidence does not lie. But it can be read too fast, so ask what else it could mean.
