TWELVE HOURS · ALL PRESENT

What the clock taught you
  1. An ORDER tells somebody to do something: “Drop me a line.” A STATEMENT only says: “Time flies.”
  2. An idiom means more than its words. “Drop me a line” is “write to me”; “kill time” is “wait”.
  3. Taken TO THE LETTER, the same words do something else. Know who is listening.
  4. A proverb is advice, and advice depends on the place: many hands for a heavy handle, one cook for one pan.
  5. FIRST, THEN, WHILE, UNTIL, BEFORE, ONCE: every one of them is about when.
  6. A pinch is not a spoonful. Twice is not three times.
  7. Better late than never.
