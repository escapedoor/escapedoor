The Keeper’s small words
  1. MAY lets you. It does not make you. SHALL and MUST do.
  2. MAY and SHALL NOT can both be kept, by not doing the thing. So “shall not” wins.
  3. UNLESS means “except if”. ONLY IF names what is needed, and promises nothing.
  4. UNTIL has an end. Ask what happens then, and whether you can make it happen.
  5. “Each leg that walks” is fewer legs than “each leg”. Read to the end of the line.
  6. “All but two” is not two. “On foot” is not on your hands.
  7. Read it before you sign it.
